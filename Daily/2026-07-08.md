- Matt Pocock teasing [Skills v1.1](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2074149038266449959): /wayfinder, /to-spec, /to-tickets, Martin-Fowlerized /code-review, /triage on external PRs — shipping this week.
- GLM 5.2 called incredible but [not truly self-hostable / not comparable to Fable](https://x.com/theo/status/2074600695454720338) — pushback on positioning claims.
- Theo's stance: [review much less of your code than 5 years ago](https://x.com/theo/status/2073917360118133191) — trust-the-agent argument, worth weighing against your own review habits.
