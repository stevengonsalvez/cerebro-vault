Debating adding a GRILL.md file to customize how /grill-me works

A few benefits:

1. /grill-with-docs gets removed, you just have 'invoke the domain-modeling skill' in your GRILL.md and use /grill-me
2. Customize what you care about/get asked about during planning (security, module strategy, etc)
3. Choose whether you want to be grilled one at a time or all together

People seem really, really reticent to actually edit the skill themselves, so this seems sensible to me.
