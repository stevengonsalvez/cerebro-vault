> Agent memory architecture, core agentic concept

AI/ML Series (Lesson 15/100):

Most AI agents fail because of the model.

That is the biggest misconception in enterprise AI.

The real failure is memory.

An AI agent that cannot remember the right information at the right time cannot reason, plan, or operate reliably.

As AI agents become autonomous, memory architecture is becoming just as important as model architecture.
