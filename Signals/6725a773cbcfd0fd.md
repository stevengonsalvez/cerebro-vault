# Memdoor: A team of 5 Qwen3.8-27B models beats Fable 5 on LiveCodeBench Hard

> Multi-model team beating single model, novel agentic coding pattern

A multi-agent harness for programming problems, written as a Memdoor workflow. Five roles (planner, two ideators, solver, manager) and a repair worker each start from a fresh context and talk only through files on disk. A shell script that runs the samples decides success, not a model. It is the method of GVS5H (Lee, 2026: five Qwen3.8-27B models match Fable 5 on LiveCodeBench Hard).
Rebuilt with the authors' exact prompts on Qwen3.8-27B, their loop scored a mean of 9.8 of 10 over five passes on the first ten LiveCodeBench Hard problems, against 9.6 for their own harness and 8.0 for Fable 5 al

## Community take
Commenters flag that this reuses the GVS5H method already posted on HN a week earlier, and suggest testing Qwen 3.8-flash-next, which they say beats the 27B model on coding and terminal benchmarks.
