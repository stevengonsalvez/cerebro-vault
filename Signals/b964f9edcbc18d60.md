> Context window vs persistent memory; core context-management topic.

an ai model can process information within its context window, but that doesn't mean it automatically retains everything for future sessions.

as work continues, conversations grow, tool results accumulate, and useful details can get buried or lost when context is compressed or a session ends.

developers have to decide what information to preserve, where to store it, and how to retrieve the right details when they're needed.

anthropic describes this broader challenge as context engineering: managing the information available to a model so it can perform a task effectively.
