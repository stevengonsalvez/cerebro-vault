# Show HN: Stroq – a local audit of what your coding agent did and why

> Audits coding agent actions; directly on-topic, local tooling.

Stroq reads your own Claude Code and Codex sessions and shows how many commands came from text your agent read, and which keys left your machine.
An example: a made-up machine with made-up sessions, keys and hosts. Hoo is Stroq's owl. Your card shows your own sessions.
npx @stroq/cli
It reads your last 30 days of sessions, your key files and the folders your agent worked in; prints key names, never values; writes nothing but a small cache under ~/.stroq; and sends nothing. No account, no telemetry.
npx @stroq/cli            a card of your own sessions, like the one above
npx @stroq/cli init

## Community take
Commenters say an eBPF backend would cover far more adversarial cases than a local command audit, and that dev work should run in a VM regardless.
