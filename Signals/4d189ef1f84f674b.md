# Harvard researchers find AI coding agents shifted the bottleneck to code review

> Coding agents shifting bottleneck to review, relevant research

Editor’s Note: Engineers at firms that adopted AI coding agents added about 30 percent more code, yet finished work did not measurably rise and getting code through review took 49 percent longer, according to a working paper by Harvard Ph.D. candidates Fiona Chen and James Stratton covering 718 firms. The paper, a job-market paper with no indication of completed peer review, measures what happens at the human checkpoint once drafting speeds up. Harvey’s motion-to-dismiss workflow and Cooley’s redaction agent, still in development with Google Cloud, both keep a lawyer at a comparable checkpoint
